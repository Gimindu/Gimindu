### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **First-Class Honours Computer Science Graduate** from the **Informatics Institute of Technology (IIT)** (affiliated with the **University of Westminster, UK**) and a versatile Full Stack Developer & AI Engineer. I have been architecting and shipping web and mobile products since I was 18.

- 💼 **Production Engineering at Scale:** Spent over a year at **Sri Lanka Telecom (SLT)** building mission-critical internal telecom software, full-stack portals, and an enterprise offline-capable Flutter application with SQLite sync.
- 🚀 **Founder & Product Builder:** Founded and engineered **[AsuraDex TCG](https://www.asuradex.com)** — a production e-commerce store with real-time pre-orders, shopping cart, and interactive 3D WebGL card interactions.
- 🧠 **Applied AI & Multimodal Systems:** Deeply invested in applied machine learning, from hybrid text & image retrieval engines (**Findeka** combining Sentence-BERT, CLIP & TextCNN) to generative AI study companions (**QuizzifyMe** powered by fine-tuned Mistral-7B).
- 🎨 **Creative Design & Fluid UX:** I believe software should be as delightful to interact with as it is robust under the hood. Obsessed with 60fps micro-interactions, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Three.js, Framer Motion, and 100% mobile-responsive design.
